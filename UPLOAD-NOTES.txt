ONHOLD HISTORY STORAGE
Historical OnHold events are stored in this browser's IndexedDB. They do not automatically sync to another computer/browser. Events already lost from all available exports and local storage cannot be reconstructed unless an old export still contains an explicit OnHold status or OnHold Time.
